      \textbf{\color{aegeanblue}Aerodynamic Engineering \& Sea Trade:}
      \vspace{0.2em}
      \begin{itemize}
          \setlength{\itemsep}{0.2em}
        \item \textbf{400-Year Industrial Operation:} Built by Venetians in the 1500s; decommissioned only mid-20th century with diesel power.
        \item \textbf{Naval Supply Infrastructure:} Milled wheat and barley into flour to manufacture \textit{paximadi} (twice-baked rusks)---an imperishable bread critical for merchant vessels across the Mediterranean.
        \item \textbf{Isotropic Cylindrical Tower:} Round 3-story masonry provides uniform resistance against omnidirectional wind shear.
        \item \textbf{Rotating Thatched Cap:} Conical timber roof rotated on grease-lubricated tracks to align sails with shifting wind vectors.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
