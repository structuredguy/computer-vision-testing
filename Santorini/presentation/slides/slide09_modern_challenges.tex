      \textbf{\color{aegeanblue}From Ancient Scarcity to Modern Strain:}
      \vspace{0.15em}
      \begin{itemize}
          \setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
        \item \textbf{Carrying Capacity Limits:} A population of $\sim$15,500 hosts nearly 3 million annual visitors. Cliff alleys face extreme bottlenecks, prompting cruise passenger caps.
        \item \textbf{The Energy-Water Nexus:} Rainwater cisterns cannot sustain peak demand. Reverse Osmosis (RO) desalination consumes $4\text{--}5\text{ kWh/m}^3$ of drinking water.
        \item \textbf{Grid Modernization:} High seasonal turbulence limits land wind turbines; future energy relies on undersea HV cables linking to the mainland grid.
      \end{itemize}
