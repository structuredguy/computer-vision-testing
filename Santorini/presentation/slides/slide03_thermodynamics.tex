      \textbf{\color{aegeanblue}Passive Environmental Controls:}
      \vspace{0.2em}
      \begin{itemize}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{0.2em}
        \item \textbf{Thermal Mass Battery:} 60--80\,cm stone walls absorb intense midday solar heat and introduce an 8--12 hour thermal lag (phase shift), radiating heat indoors only during chilly nights.
        \item \textbf{High-Albedo Envelope:} Annual lime wash (\textit{asvesti}) yields solar reflectance $>0.80$, reflecting shortwave radiation before it penetrates the stone.
        \item \textbf{Natural Antiseptic:} Slaked lime served as an organic disinfectant during historic cholera and plague outbreaks.
        \item \textbf{Venturi Fenestration:} Minimal, deeply recessed south openings paired with north vents induce natural stack-effect cross-ventilation.
      \end{itemize}
